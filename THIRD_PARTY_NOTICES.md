## @remotion/sfx sounds

`nolan setup` vendors a small allowlist of sounds from [`@remotion/sfx`](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/sfx) (4.0.530) into `public/sfx/`: whoosh, whip, mouseClick (`click`), uiSwitch (`switch`), pageTurn (`page-turn`), shutterModern (`shutter`). Each of these is marked CC0 on its remotion.dev page (sources in `public/sfx/credits.json`). `ding` is **not** vendored: its docs page says it is "not explicitly released under a free license", so `setup` synthesizes our own ding instead.

`ding`, `riser`, `impact`, `pop` (`public/sfx/*.mp3`) and `public/music/fallback-bed.mp3` are generated by `nolan setup` with ffmpeg and are dedicated to the public domain (CC0) by this project. `@remotion/sfx` is part of Remotion and is subject to the Remotion license (https://www.remotion.dev/license).

## Media fetched at runtime

Music and SFX downloaded by `nolan audio` (Openverse, ccMixter, Freesound) keep their own licenses (CC0, public domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA on opt-in). Each file is recorded with its license and attribution in `public/projects/<slug>/assets.lock.json`, and credited in the video's outro.
